# Open Questions & Decisions Required

The following must be confirmed by the APC Stakeholders Congress before production:

## Organization

1. Exact legal/official name of the organization.
2. Official logo and brand assets.
3. Exact title of the approving officer: Director, DG, Coordinator, etc.
4. Full name of authorized signatory.
5. Whether the signatory's signature may be digitally applied.
6. Official stamp/seal asset and authorization.
7. Official registration/document numbering convention.

## Verification

8. Who reviews registrations?
9. Is approval one-stage or State + National?
10. What makes a forum eligible for verification?
11. Which documents are mandatory?
12. What happens when information is false?
13. Can verification be revoked?

## Geography

14. Exact list/source for Kwara LGAs and wards.
15. Whether a forum may operate in multiple LGAs.
16. Whether "Kwara State at Large" requires multiple LGA/ward selections.

## Data

17. Is member-level data actually required in MVP?
18. Is NIN actually required? If yes, why and through what lawful/approved verification process?
19. Is bank information required at launch? If no, remove it.
20. What exact member-list columns will be accepted in Excel?

## Documents

21. Exact wording of Certificate of Registration.
22. Exact wording of Letter of Recognition.
23. Whether both documents are issued only after final verification.
24. Whether certificates require expiry dates.
25. Whether a QR verification page is desired.

## Communications

26. Official email sender.
27. Official WhatsApp Business/API arrangement, if any.
28. SMS provider and budget.
29. Whether recipients have opted into bulk communications.

## Public directory

30. Which forum information is public?
31. Should WhatsApp links be public?
32. Should a forum have a public profile page?

## Legal/privacy

33. Approved privacy notice.
34. Data retention policy.
35. Data subject request process.
36. Terms/disclaimer.
37. Responsible privacy/compliance contact.

## Branding

38. Official brand colors/fonts.
39. Approved hero imagery.
40. Social media handles.
41. Official website/domain.
